Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

In the Dream Ocean tab, tap a box you like and choose Bring to My Workshop. In your workshop, answer the question for the current step and the tooth is done. When every tooth is complete, you can open the box.

Can I add my own dream?

Yes. In your workshop, tap Add New Dream and enter a title, a category and the steps to get there.

How do I pause or restart a dream?

Open the menu on a dream in progress and choose Give Up This Dream. It moves to Paused Dreams in the treasury, where you can tap Restart to bring it back to your workshop or Delete Forever to remove it.

What is Climb Mode?

Climb Mode lets you manage dreams as mountains to climb. Turn it on or off in Settings › Display. It adds step deadlines and obstacles such as cost and time, so you can track a dream like a project.

Some photos don't load.

Some background photos are downloaded from the internet. Check that you're connected. Without a connection, the app shows a default image instead.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your data is stored only on your device, with no account or sync. Restoring a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings it along with your other app data.

Are there any paid features?

No. Every feature is free.
