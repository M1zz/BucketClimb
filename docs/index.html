Questions

How do I get started?

On the Dream Ocean tab, tap a box you like and choose Bring to My Workshop. In My Workshop, answer the questions on the current step and the teeth fill in one by one. Fill them all to open the box.

Can I add my own dream?

Yes. In My Workshop, tap Add New Dream and enter a title, an area and the steps to get there.

How do I pause a dream or pick it back up?

In an active dream's menu, choose Give Up This Dream and it moves to Paused Dreams in the Treasury. Tap Restart there to bring it back to My Workshop, or Delete Forever to remove it.

What is Climb Mode?

A screen that manages your dreams like climbing a mountain. Turn it on or off in Settings > Display. You can track deadlines per step and obstacles like cost and time, project-style.

Photos aren't showing.

Some dream backgrounds are downloaded from the internet. Check that you're online. Without a connection, a default picture is shown.

Can I move my notes to a new iPhone?

Your notes are stored only on this device, with no account or sync. Restoring a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup brings them along with your other app data.

Is there anything to buy inside the app?

No. Once you have the app, every feature is yours. The tips in Settings are only there if you'd like to say thanks.

Are my dreams and notes sent anywhere?

No. There's no account, and no ads or analytics. Your dreams, notes and photos stay on your device.

How do I contact you?

Bug reports, ideas, anything at all. Send them from Send Feedback in the app's Settings, or by Instagram DM (@lee25_ios).
